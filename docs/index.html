Our fishing model

Peaks one hour before and after high tide, with a boost near new and full moons.

How the score works

Fishing score = tide points × moon weight

Tide points are 90 one hour before or after the nearest high tide, 81 at high tide and two hours away, 72 three hours away, 63 four hours away, and 54 five hours away. The curve connects these points linearly and continues at the same rate beyond five hours.

Moon weight = 1 − (distance to the nearest new or full moon in days × 0.06). The weight drops by 6 percentage points per day. The final score ranges from 0% to 90%.

Use the score to compare fishing times. Daily peaks include overnight hours. Weather is shown alongside the score.

How the cod score works

Cod score = 30 + 60 × (Tide & Moon score ÷ 90) × sun weight.

Sun weight is 100% within 30 minutes of sunrise or sunset. It tapers linearly to zero at three hours from the nearer event. The combined score ranges from 30 to 90 points. Sunrise and sunset use St. John’s local time.

Weather & wind categories

Rain outlook: Clear, Drizzle or Rain. Clear covers rain-free hours, including cloudy or foggy skies. Snow, freezing conditions and thunder are flagged when forecast.

Wind: Light below 15 km/h; Moderate from 15 to below 30 km/h; Strong at 30 km/h or more. Levels use mean wind speed; gusts are listed separately.

The hourly forecast covers up to 16 days. Day summaries show the forecast at each model’s peak. In Cod mode, Week and Day also show forecasts for sunrise and sunset.
